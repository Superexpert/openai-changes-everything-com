Published June 29, 2026.

Young people are already struggling to get their first real job. Now AI may be removing the entry-level work that used to help people start a career.

Stephen Walther talks with Kevin Frazier, who leads the AI Innovation and Law Program at UT Austin. They discuss what AI means for young workers, why the first rung of the career ladder may be at risk, and what policies might prevent the future of work from becoming dystopian.
